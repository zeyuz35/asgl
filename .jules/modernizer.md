## 2025-05-13 - Replace CVXPY Element-Wise Multiplications with Inner Products
**Learning:** In CVXPY, `cp.sum(cp.multiply(A, B))` creates extremely large expression trees which severely degrades canonicalization time (the time `cvxpy` takes to compile the problem data before sending it to the underlying solver). Replacing this with a simple vector dot product `A @ B` (and `cp.norm1(cp.multiply(weights, beta))` with `cp.sum(weights.T @ cp.abs(beta))`) solves the identical mathematical problem but is vastly more efficient for CVXPY to canonicalize. Note that this optimization applies to strictly non-negative weight vectors to maintain DCP convexity compliance. Do not touch `cp.sum_squares(cp.multiply(A, B))` as `cp.sum_squares` optimizes its canonicalization differently and vectorizing it with `A**2 @ cp.square(B)` degrades actual solver performance.
**Action:** Always prefer `@` for vector inner products over `cp.sum(cp.multiply(..., ...))` when dealing with CVXPY variables and non-negative constant arrays.
