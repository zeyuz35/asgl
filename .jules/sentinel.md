## 2024-05-01 - [CVXPY canonicalization backend injection]
**Vulnerability:** The `canon_backend` parameter in `Regressor` was directly passed to the underlying `cvxpy.Problem.solve` without validation. While CVXPY implements some internal checks, a bare `try-except` block mapping standard Python `Exception`s or `ValueError`s can swallow unexpected errors from deeper levels (like missing backends or unsafe imports dynamically loaded by a backend), obscuring the root cause and potentially allowing unintended execution paths or denial of service if bad input is repeatedly provided.
**Learning:** Even though hyperparameter validation in local ML wrappers does not typically constitute a severe remote injection vulnerability, it is a crucial defense-in-depth practice. Unvalidated string inputs passed to complex dependencies (like solver backends) can trigger internal exceptions that are poorly handled, creating operational vulnerabilities or unexpected test/runtime failures.
**Prevention:** Implement explicit allowlists (e.g., `ALLOWED_CANON_BACKENDS = {"CPP", "SCIPY", "COO"}`) and validate string parameters strictly at the wrapper boundary (`_check_attributes`) before they interact with internal dependencies.

## 2024-05-15 - [Missing numeric validation for adaptive weights]
**Vulnerability:** The numeric hyperparameters `weight_tol` and `variability_pct` in the `AdaptiveWeights` class were instantiated without proper domain validation. Specifically, `weight_tol` being allowed to be 0.0 or negative creates a significant risk for `ZeroDivisionError` when individual weights (`1 / (tmp_weight**self.individual_power_weight + self.weight_tol)`) or group weights are calculated. `variability_pct` > 1.0 would cause unexpected behavior and cryptic failure when passed down to scikit-learn's PCA.
**Learning:** For mathematical robustness and to prevent runtime exceptions like division by zero or out-of-bounds array lookups, numeric tolerance limits and percentage thresholds must be explicitly validated during instantiation or immediately before consumption in mathematical operations. Local wrapper validation is a crucial layer of defense even if dependencies have their own checks.
**Prevention:** Implement `sklearn.utils.validation.check_scalar` for `weight_tol` (requiring > 0.0) and `variability_pct` (requiring <= 1.0) early in `fit_weights` before accessing internal functions or performing inverse weighting logic.
