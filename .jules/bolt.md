## 2024-05-24 - Inner products for CVXPY performance
**Learning:** In CVXPY, `cp.sum(cp.multiply(A, B))` and `cp.norm1(cp.multiply(A, B))` can take significant time to canonicalize (compile) when the dimensions are large. Replacing these operations with the inner product `A @ B` drastically speeds up compilation time without affecting the solution. However, when the output needs to be a scalar and matrix dimensions don't naturally collapse, we must still wrap the inner product in `cp.sum()`.
**Action:** Use inner products for constant/variable elementwise products summed over, but ensure constant arrays are wrapped in `np.abs()` when multiplying convex expressions like `cp.abs()` to satisfy CVXPY's DCP rules. Wrap the inner product with `cp.sum()` if it results in an array instead of a scalar objective.
