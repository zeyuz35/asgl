## YYYY-MM-DD - Inner Product Canonicalization Optimization
**Learning:** CVXPY compilation and solving times for large optimization problems can be significantly reduced by reformulating element-wise multiplication inside aggregations (cp.multiply(A, B)) into matrix inner products (A.T @ B or A @ B). This is true for logistic regression objectives and various penalizations (Group Lasso, Adaptive Ridge, Adaptive Lasso). For matrix variables, reshaping or appropriately projecting onto axes is required to maintain correct DCP semantics while using inner products.
**Action:** When working on CVXPY-based estimators, check if cp.multiply followed by a summation or norm can be translated into an inner product to optimize canonicalization performance without changing the mathematical objective.
